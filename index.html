About Me

I'm a data analyst with a focus on healthcare and financial analysis, drawn to the challenge of taking confusing, scattered information and turning it into a clear story.

My journey started at HiiT Plc, where i was kept in the hands of experts and i was thoroughly drilled, then i moved to watching YouTube, and paid for a course on Udemy, before I worked with real-world data as an intern at DecodeLabs Tech, where I built a sales dashboard analysis.
